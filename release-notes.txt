HealthMate — Release Notes

Sharing
- Share your food log with friends and family — tap Share on the dashboard to give someone instant access by email.
- Request to view someone else's log — they'll get a request to approve next time they open the app.
- Switch between your log and connected users' logs with the dropdown in the header — view their calories, macros, meals, and photos inline.
- Remove connections anytime from the Share sheet or user switcher (with confirmation).
- Works with emails not yet on the platform — the connection activates automatically when they sign up.

Before you share
- First-time sharers see a quick profile setup — upload a photo and set your name so others can identify you easily.
- Skippable if you're in a hurry.

Account
- Bottom nav now shows a clean icon instead of your profile photo.

Photos
- Failed photo identifications no longer lose your image — retry anytime (works even after a refresh), straight from a Retry button on the error.
- Failed photos now stay neatly in the top photo tray instead of cluttering meal sections.

Logging
- The Log Food screen is cleaner after you log — the input box hides so you can review your items without extra scrolling.
- Quantity fields can now be fully cleared while editing.
- If we can't parse your entry, your typed text is restored so you don't have to retype.

Polish
- Food cards no longer slide open by accident while scrolling.
- Bottom sheets stay fully on-screen on mobile (titles no longer scroll out of view).
